# 💻 Welcome to **JSQUARE Learning Program**

### 🎓 Junior Programming Skill Training

*(Part of JSDEVBRAINS DIGITAL PRIVATE LIMITED)*

Dear Parents,

Welcome to the **JSQUARE Learning Program**, proudly powered by **JSDEVBRAINS DIGITAL PRIVATE LIMITED**.
This program is designed to help children develop **strong programming and logical thinking skills** step-by-step — in a **fun, engaging, and structured** way.

Your child will go through a **12-month coding journey**, and **every two months**, they will receive a **performance-based certificate** 🏅 that recognizes their effort, creativity, and learning progress.

Each certificate comes with a **special color code**, representing the child’s achievement level and development stage. 🌈

---

## 🗓️ **Program Overview**

| **Duration** | **Milestones**           | **Certificate**                      | **Goal**                                      |
| ------------ | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| 12 Months    | 6 Levels (2 Months Each) | Performance-Based Color Certificates | Build real programming skills with confidence |

Each stage includes **interactive coding lessons, practice activities, and a mini-project**, with certificates awarded based on **performance, participation, and creativity**.

Every **two months**, students are evaluated based on:

* 🌟 Creativity and problem-solving effort

Certificates will be awarded with remarks such as:

## 🚀 **Learning Journey**

Your child will:

1. Attend weekly hands-on programming sessions.
2. Learn by writing and running real code in **Visual Studio**.
3. Build simple logic and problem-solving habits early.
4. Complete mini-projects every 2 months.
5. Earn a colorful certificate for each completed stage.

At the end of 12 months, they will proudly receive the
🏆 **Grand Master Certificate** — showing completion of the full program!
