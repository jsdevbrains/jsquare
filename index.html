Frequently Asked Questions

What are the eligibility criteria for the programs? expand_more

Our programs are open to final year students and recent graduates from any engineering or computer science background. A basic understanding of programming is recommended but not mandatory for all courses.

Is there a placement guarantee? expand_more

We provide comprehensive placement assistance, including resume building, mock interviews, and access to our network of partner companies. While we have a high placement rate, we do not offer a 100% guarantee as it depends on individual performance and market conditions.

What is the duration of the training programs? expand_more

Our programs typically range from 3 to 6 months, depending on the course and specialization. They are designed to be intensive and immersive to get you job-ready in the shortest possible time.
